WebGPU states, using the instrument’s zoom, pan and framing controls. The Fit
captures deliberately show the entire world and its aspect ratio. Field views
use 1,000 updates; region charts contain explicit samples at updates 100, 200
and 300. Exact capture procedure: `scripts/evolution-screenshots.mjs`.
