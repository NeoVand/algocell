## 1. The system

Algocell is an artificial-life "soup" of Z80 programs in the tradition of Computational Life (Agüera y Arcas et al. 2024, BFF/Z80 soups, no fitness function) and the ablation follow-up by Cicala et al. 2026 (arXiv 2607.09211, removed copy instructions). Ours:
- 20,000 cells (160 × 125, square grid), each a tape of L bytes. L ∈ {4, 9, 16, 25, 36, 49, 64, 81, 100} have been run; shaders now exist for L ∈ {3…100} incl. {8, 10, 12, 18, 20, 24, 32, 50}.
- Each step draws 8,192 random (cell, 4-neighbour) pairs; pairs whose cells are already claimed are dropped; measured on the GPU 3,982 ± 35 pairs run per step (48.6%), i.e. 0.40 interactions per cell per step.
- A pair's two tapes are concatenated into one 2L-byte Z80 memory with addresses wrapping mod 2L (a ring); registers zero; SP = largest 16-bit value aliasing the last byte; the Z80 runs `z80_steps` instructions (each LDIR iteration is one step; HALT forfeits the rest); both tapes are written back.
- Mutation: 8192/2^k random soup bytes replaced per step (k = 4 → 512), independent of L; per cell one byte every 2^k·2.44 steps; per-byte rate ∝ 1/L. A non-atomic read-modify-write drops ≈0.3% of mutations at L = 16, k = 4 (≈5% at L = 4, k = 2).
- Instruction ablation: whole opcode sets execute as NOPs (prefix-aware; suppressed base op = 1-byte NOP, CB/ED = 2-byte). Patterns like `family:block-copy`, `PUSH`, `-RLD`. The Z80 core is the `@neovand/zilion` 0.2.0 WGSL package; the headless Python runner (wgpu-py) executes the shader text exported from the app byte-for-byte.
- GPU races (atomic claim order) make runs non-deterministic: the same seed diverges within hundreds of steps. Each run is one sample.
- Compute: Modal L40S, ≈0.30 ms/step at 128 Z80 steps (300k steps ≈ 90 s ≈ $0.05 + ≈$0.01 overhead); 512 steps ≈ 0.85 ms/step; 1M steps ≈ $0.16. 50 concurrent containers.

## 2. What is measured per run today (`algocell_exp/run.py`)
Per sample (every 500 steps in A/B; in C–E every 50 steps to 5,000 then 500): exact-genotype species stats (unique, top share, top-3 hashes/shares, entropy, Simpson), quasispecies occupancy within Hamming radius ⌈L/4⌉ of the dominant tape (`q_share`), shift-invariant occupancy (`q_shift_share`, best cyclic shift ≥ 0.75, 2,000-cell subsample), motif share, byte entropy H0 and brotli high-order entropy, byte-pattern census (cells containing ED A0/A8/B0/B8; ≥2 PUSH bytes; E3; FF; LD (HL),x; ≥8 zero bytes; CB-page (HL) ops; top five 4-grams), the top-3 exemplar tapes with mechanism labels under the run's suppression, 256-bin byte histogram and zero-byte fraction, active pairs in the last step, 8 uniformly random cell tapes, elapsed time. Full soup snapshots (brotli) only at the first `q_share ≥ 0.10` crossing and at the end. Condition record carries provenance (shader/ISA hashes, adapter, library versions, git commit, launch time).

Post hoc (`assay_batch.py`, local GPU): the replication assay executes a tape as program A against 64 random partners (or in-situ partners from a snapshot) for the run's budget and suppression: `score` = headroom-normalised gain in best-cyclic-shift similarity of the partner to the tape; `gen2_score` = the same for the offspring it produced (two-generation yield); `gen2_cond` (heritability given a copy); `offspring_within_q` (share of partners that became ≥75% copies); `self_preserved_as_A/B`. Replicator := gen2 ≥ 0.3 (pre-registered); faithful := additionally offspring_within_q ≥ 0.5. Per run: `t_rep` (first sample where a top-3 exemplar with share ≥0.5% is heritable), `t_faith`, emergence-time exemplar block, final block (best final exemplar, random and in-situ), functional fraction of 32 random final cells (random and in-situ partners), tolerant periods, mechanisms. Null: 200 random tapes gen2 max 0.03; all-NOP tape gen2 0.13 (partners push zeros).
Succession (`succession.py`): census takeover times (30% headroom-normalised excess over per-L random baselines), LDIR invasion 10%→90% (censored), family at 5k/50k/300k and at the last step, zero load. Zoo (`zoo.py`): design signatures (one instruction period ×n for tiled tapes). Figures via `figstyle.py`; numbers via `findings.py` → NUMBERS.md.

## 3. What has been run and found (1,270 runs; exploratory status)
Stage A (630, L = 16): 7 ablations × steps {32,128,512} × k {2,4,6} × seeds 1–10, 300k steps, early stop 4 samples after q_share ≥ 0.5. Stage B (640): L ∈ {4,9,25,…,100} × {none, block-copy, stack-writes, no-copy} × {128,512} × k=4 × seeds 1–10.
Findings (details: `results/stageB/FINDINGS.md`, `results/stageB/NUMBERS.md`, `runs/REPORT_stageA.md`):
1. Unablated: heritable replicator in 10/10 seeds at every L ≥ 16 (faithful 10/10 except 8/10 at L = 100 · 128 steps, one more censored by the early stop) and every budget/mutation cell at L = 16; KM median 500–1,500 steps at 128 steps (500 = sampling floor). Budget is the strongest knob (32 steps: KM medians 10k–70k). L = 4: 1/80. L = 9: 4/10 and 1/10.
2. Removing the 46-opcode "stack-writes" arm (stack + exchange + call/return + RST; 24 of them write nothing): emergence 20–100× slower at L = 16 at 128/512 steps (2–3× at 32), always LDIR shift-copiers; at 128 steps and L = 81/100 these are heritable but unfaithful (2/7; 0/7 with 3 censored by the early stop) because one LDIR iteration copies one byte per step. Block-copy removal: no effect except 32 steps × 1/4 mutation (0/10 vs 10/10) and L = 9 (0/10). no-copy (112 removed): 0/250; rmw-only: 0/90.
3. Tiling: first replicators have period 2 at every L ≥ 16 (Load–Push `01 c5` / `21 e3`); LDIR replicators' periods divide 2L in 90–100% of runs (gcd law verified with the executor: shift-copy on a ring of 2L converges to period gcd(offset, 2L)). HOE 0.6–2.6 bits/byte under the stack family, 3.4–6.0 under LDIR. Whole-tape copiers (period L, exact copy, high-entropy cargo) exist but stay rare: 6 faithful final exemplars at L = 49–81 at 0.06–0.2% share, 3 as first replicators. Minimal replicator seen: 3 bytes (`DEC E ; LDIR`).
4. L = 9 reversal: removing the stack arm raises emergence to 9/10 and 10/10 (one-sided p = 0.029, 6e-5). Zero floods are the primordial state at every L (24–34% zeros at the emergence crossing at 128 steps, 17–26% at 512); the stack doubles the flood (≈ 0.19 remains with the 22 writing stack opcodes removed); at L ≥ 16 a stack replicator rises out of it; at L = 9 none does.
5. Measurement: dominant exact genotype usually sterile product of a copier; occupancy (tq_10) and compression measures misclassify ≈11% of runs (floods, diverse clouds); CALL chain `cd xx` heritable for 2 generations then dies (gen2 0.49, copies 0%); RST smear copies 64% but gen2 0.09.
6. Succession: stack family first (1.5–3k steps), LDIR invasions later depending on budget/mutation; parity of L drives the early stop (even L phase-lock and stop at 3–5k steps, odd L run to 300k).

The owner asked whether our findings bear on biology/astrobiology; the current answer (see conversation summary in `RESEARCH_DIRECTIONS.md` §5 and the 'biology' discussion): candidate bridges are (i) pattern/complexity-based life detection fails where a functional assay succeeds (biosignature debate, assembly theory); (ii) repeat structure dictated by copy geometry (rolling-circle replication, viroids, tandem repeats); (iii) Eigen's error threshold solved by redundancy rather than fidelity (size decouples from information); (iv) a cheap sterile spreading process can pre-empt emergence (the "weed"/tar problem) so ablation can create life; (v) emergence is threshold-like on the existence of any copy-capable primitive; (vi) heritable-but-unfaithful lineages assembled across encounters (multipartite/collective replication).

## 7. Measured while the review runs (2026-10-07, local GPU/CPU)
- Full-soup snapshot sizes (brotli q5, 20,000 cells): L = 100 zero-flooded no-copy soup 684 KB (22 ms), L = 100 stack-tiled soup 214 KB (16 ms), L = 100 LDIR cloud 52 KB (4 ms), L = 16 soups 59–96 KB (3–4 ms); a truly random L = 100 soup does not compress (2.0 MB). A log-spaced schedule {500, 1k, 2k, 3k, 5k, 7.5k, 10k, 15k, 20k, 30k, 50k, 75k, 100k, 150k, 200k, 300k (+500k, 750k, 1M)} gives 16–19 snapshots per run, i.e. ≈ 1–12 MB per run, ≈ 10 GB for all of C–E worst case.
- Interaction census prototype (scratch copy of soup.py with COPY_SRC on the pair buffers; no shader change): after a step, `pair_data` holds the post-execution, pre-mutation state of every pair; it equals the written-back soup except for that step's ≈200 mutated bytes. Reading pairs (64 KB) + active flags (32 KB) + pair_data (1.6 MB at L = 100) and computing best-shift similarity for all ≈3,950 active pairs takes ≈ 4 ms (L = 16) to 66 ms (L = 100) of CPU plus ≈ 50 ms of readback; if the census runs on the last step of a sampling interval it needs one extra soup read (the "before" state). Measured on step 3,001 of unablated soups: copy events (partner became ≥ 75% similar to the program) 6.1% of interactions at L = 16 and 13.0% at L = 100; mean partner bytes written 1.2 (L = 16) and 18.4 (L = 100); zeros were 17–20% of bytes written under the full ISA and 1% with the stack arm removed. Running it at every sample would add ≈ 50% to the runtime of an L = 100 run; at the fine early samples plus every 2,500 steps it adds ≈ 15%.
- Life-detector benchmark prototype on the 1,270 final soups (ground truth "alive" = ≥ 25% of 32 random final cells are heritable replicators against random partners; 658/1,270 alive). AUC of pattern-based detectors: high-order entropy (the Computational Life paper's indicator) 0.90 overall but **1.00 at L = 16 and 0.53 at L ≥ 25**; brotli bits/byte 0.97; byte entropy H0 0.78; top exact share 0.82; quasispecies share 0.74; unique-tape fraction 0.98; species entropy 0.97; motif share 0.63; zero-flood census 0.37 (points the wrong way). 186 runs are structured-but-dead (HOE > 1 bit, no replicators: every no-copy run at L ≥ 25, i.e. zero floods), 27 are alive-but-unstructured (HOE < 1 bit with replicators: odd-L stack soups split into two phase variants). So the field's standard indicator works in the regime it was developed in (L = 16) and fails outside it; the diversity-collapse detectors (unique fraction, species entropy) are the best pattern-based proxies but all are blind to *which* cells are alive. Time-resolved versions need the log-spaced snapshots.
