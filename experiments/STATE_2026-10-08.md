## 1. What this is

A Nature Article, "Heredity before individuality in a digital primordial soup" (`manuscript/MAIN_nature.md`), about soups
of random Z80 programs (20,000 tapes on a 160 × 125 square lattice, neighbours execute each other, no fitness function).
Thesis, in one line: the first replicator is an open two-byte literal (`01 c5` = `LD BC,nn ; PUSH BC`, it writes its own
bytes and runs on into its neighbour); closure (a cycle in control flow, not a membrane) evolves later; a second machine
(BFF) without a literal channel is born closed and with one added instruction is born open; whether the sterile "tar"
(zero bytes) is lethal decides how the open phase ends. Theorems: closure requires a cycle; information inflow I(o;e|x)
falls from a median 7 bits to 0 across the transition. Everything is pre-registered, measured by intervention (culture
test, partner tests), and traceable to generated tables (`manuscript/NUMBERS_INDEX.md`).
