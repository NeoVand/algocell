## Thesis: self-replication before the self

1. **Replication begins without a self.**
   - The pusher executes its partner: it runs off its own end and continues in the other tape, running its own
     pattern in the other's body.
   - The unit that replicates is a form spread across tapes, not the cell the simulation provides. Analogies: crystal
     growth, prion conversion.
   - Lemma 11 (open straight-line code fetches every byte) means nothing in it is kept: all is used.
   - X1 (0 of 512 mutants carried) is the empirical face of this. X2 (inflow, pointer in the partner 80 of 80) shows
     the permeability.
2. **The self is born as a boundary of control (no membrane).** Closure, meaning a refusal to execute the other,
   creates three selves at once:
   - a control self (Theorem 2: loops);
   - an informational self (Lemma 7: fidelity ⇒ zero inflow);
   - a hereditary self (Lemma 10 + Proposition 12: genome = copied but never executed, d − c sites).

   Candidate contribution to the theory of individuality: in stored-program matter, the competing criteria can be
   computed exactly, and they arise together at one transition. The criteria are autopoiesis (operational closure),
   Krakauer et al. (informational autonomy), Godfrey-Smith (Darwinian individuality) and Pradeu
   (immunological self/non-self).
3. **The self sets its own size,** and the self comes before the genome.
   - Copy period d is the extent of the self, and d − c is the genome. Both are set by one byte of its own code, not by
     the simulation's cell.
   - Regenerators are selves with almost no genome (≤ 2 sites); transmitters are selves with a genome.
   - The ladder: **pattern → self (limited heredity) → self with genome.**
4. **The starting rung is set by the substrate's chemistry and by the environment's danger.**
   - **Chemistry:** literal-channel width.
     - The corrected theory: width 1 cannot write its own instruction stream as a periodic tiling (proved for any
       one-register mixture); width ≥ 2 can.
     - Z80 (2): the pattern rung. 6502 (1): the self rung, predicted and observed.
     - 8086 (2): the pattern rung, predicted, as the third registered test.
   - **Danger:** the lethality dial. When executing foreign zeros is lethal, open-first falls from 10 of 10 to 2 of 10.
     The self arises at once, as a refusal to execute foreign code: a primordial immunity.
5. **Selves are built by the selfless phase.**
   - Founders arise by rewrite, written by other programs (N3/N6, open-first on every line).
   - Hypothesis: the pattern phase is the midwife of individuals.
   - The 6502, with no pattern phase, closes about 50× later than the Z80. That is confounded by the Z80's easier loops
     (LDIR).
   - **Within-machine test, perhaps in existing data:** do the Z80 ablation arms that remove the literal channel close
     like the 6502?
