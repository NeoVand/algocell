# Stage E — the size axis under control (1,230 runs, 2026-10-07)

Design (PLAN.md Stage E, specified before any run): `none` and `stack-write-only`, 128 steps, mutation 1/16, **seeds 101–110**, no early stop, 300,000-step horizon, explicit early samples, full snapshots and interaction census on the log schedule. Arms: **@nominal** at L ∈ {3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 12, 16, 18, 20, 24, 25, 32, 36, 49, 50, 64, 81, 100}; **@mubyte** (32·L mutated bytes per step — the L = 16 per-byte rate at every L), **@bytes** (320,000 soup bytes at every L: 80,000 cells at L = 4 … 3,200 at L = 100), **@steps8L** (8·L Z80 steps per encounter), each at the nine square L; **@musweep** (L = 100, 128 steps, mutation 1/2 … 1/256); **@budget** (L = 100, 32–2,048 steps); **@var** (10 repeats of seed 1 in three cells); **@x32k2** (E7: `none` vs `block-copy` at 32 steps · 1/4, seeds 1001–1020). 1,230/1,230 runs completed, 0 failures, median 98 s per run (max 709 s). The interaction clock is 0.39–0.40 active interactions per cell per step in every arm, @bytes included, so steps are quoted. Numbers: `NUMBERS.md`, `stage_e/NUMBERS_E.md` (+ `size_arms.csv`, `budget_L100.csv`, `mutation_L100.csv`, `unit_fitness_vs_L.csv`), `ZOO.md`, `cells.csv`, `assays.csv`.

## Where life starts, and the dead zone at L = 9–12

From `none`@nominal, the zoo and an isolated-assay sweep (`stage_e/unit_fitness_vs_L.csv`: the canonical units tiled to each L, executed against 64 random partners for 128 or 32 steps):

- **L = 3, 4, 7: nothing** in 300,000 steps (0/10 each; random-cell replicator fraction 0). The 3-byte unit `1d ed b0` (DEC E ; LDIR) is perfectly heritable in isolation at L = 3 (gen2 1.00) yet never assembles in the soup; @mubyte at L = 4 (per-byte rate of L = 16) is also 0/10, so the per-byte mutation rate alone is not the reason.
- **L = 5: 9/10** (KM 49,000), all whole-tape copiers (`c3 1d ed b0 4e` and relatives: JP nn ; LDIR; period 5 = L). **L = 6: 9/10** (KM 151,500), period-3 and period-6 LDIR/LDDR units (4/10 faithful).
- **L = 8: 10/10 at KM 500**, the pusher `01 c5 ×4` in 10/10 (all faithful).
- **L = 9, 10, 12: 4/10, 4/10, 6/10**, all LDIR units (`1d ed b0 ×3`; period-5 units at L = 10; periods 4, 6, 8, 12 at L = 12); the pusher appears once (L = 10, gen2 0.31). The unresolved runs are floods: zero fraction 0.33 (L = 10, 6/10 runs) and 0.35 (L = 12, 4/10), random-cell replicator fraction 0.
- **L ≥ 16** (16, 18, 20, 24, 25, 32, 36, 49, 50, 64, 81, 100): 10/10, the pusher first (modal period 2 at every L).

The isolated assay explains the shape: the pusher's heritability is 0.39 (L = 4), 0.04 (6), 0.07 (7), **0.53 (8)**, 0.32 (9), 0.31 (10), **0.20 (12)**, 0.59 (16) and 0.43–0.72 from 18 to 100 — marginal or below the 0.3 threshold exactly at L = 9–12 (and at 6–7), where the soup fails, and healthy at L = 8 and L ≥ 16, where it wins. It tolerates truncated tiling (0.61 at L = 25, 0.58 at 49, 0.71 at 81), whereas the LDIR units need exact tiling (`1d ed b0`: 1.00 at L ∈ {3, 5, 6, 9, 18, 24}, 0.83 at 36, ≈ 0 at most other L; `04 5e ed b0`: 1.00 at multiples of 4 up to 64). The dead zone is a copy-geometry property of the unit on the 2L ring, not of the mutation rate (@mubyte at L = 9: 1/10), the soup size (@bytes: 6/10) or the budget (@steps8L: 2/10). At 32 steps the pusher's isolated heritability is 0.54 at L = 16 (it falls to 0.26 at 32 and 0.03 at 100) — yet no 32-step · 1/4 soup has ever produced it (Stage C, E7): the 32-step regime selects LDIR for a reason other than the pusher's intrinsic fitness (open).

## Budget at L = 100: a heritability floor and a sub-clonal regime (E4)

`none`: at **32 steps** nothing (0/10; final top exemplar gen2 ≤ 0.25; random-cell replicator fraction 0; 8/10 soups degenerate to 0.5 bits/byte with zero fraction 0.21). At **64 steps** the pre-registered event never fires (0/10), but **6/10 final soups are heritable populations without a clone**: the top genotype's share is ≤ 0.07%, the top exemplar's gen2 is 0.35–0.61, 69–100% of random cells are heritable (gen2 ≥ 0.3), none is faithful, and the high-order entropy is 3.7–5.8 bits/byte; the other 3/10 are floods (zero fraction 0.34) and 1/10 is mixed. `stack-write-only` at 64 steps: 6/10 the same (100% of random cells heritable, top share ≤ 0.03%), 4/10 partial (6–50%). The event `t_rep` requires a top-3 exemplar with ≥ 0.5% share — a clonality condition. At 64 steps a pusher writes 32 of 100 bytes per encounter, so heritability is carried by the population (partial copies accumulate over encounters) with no genotype dominating. From 128 steps up the clonal regime returns (10/10; KM 1,650 at 128 → 450 at 256 → 150 at 1,024 → 100 at 2,048 under `none`; faithful 10/10 from 256).

## Mutation at L = 100 (E5)

Under `none` the pusher is heritable in 9–10/10 runs at every rate from 1.6 × 10⁻⁵ to 2.0 × 10⁻³ mutations per byte per step, with KM medians 950 → 12,000 steps and faithfulness falling only at the highest rate (2/10). Under `stack-write-only` the LDIR regime shows the error-threshold curve: the median period of the first heritable unit is 32.5, 22.5, 15, 8, 5, 5 bytes from the lowest to the highest rate, the final high-order entropy falls from 6.0 to 3.3 bits/byte, and faithful replicators exist only at the lowest rate (7/10) and marginally at 1/16 (1/10).

## Variance and reproducibility (E6)

Ten repeats of the same seed are not the same trajectory: at `none` L = 16 they emerge at 150 steps (KM) with a spread of 0.25 dex against 0.46 dex across seeds; at `stack-writes` L = 16, 8/10 vs 8/10 with 0.38 vs 0.45 dex; at `none` L = 9, 3/10 vs 4/10. The pair-claim race in the shader (which of two colliding pairs wins) makes the GPU non-deterministic, so a seed fixes the initial soup and the mutation stream but not the history. Consequences: seeds are exchangeable replicates and all per-cell statistics in Stages A–E stand; no individual run can be reproduced bit-for-bit, and claims about particular runs must be population claims.

## What this settles and what it does not

Settled: above L = 16 self-replication emerges in every seed in every control arm, and the residual growth of emergence time with L is a budget-per-byte effect, not a mutation-rate or lottery effect; the 2L divisor law holds in every arm; there is no monotone size floor — the pusher regime exists at L = 8 and from L = 16 up and is absent at L = 9–12 where the unit's own heritability is marginal; life exists from L = 5 (whole-tape copiers) and is absent at L = 3, 4, 7; the LDIR regime obeys an error-threshold curve (maintained period 5 → 32.5 bytes over two decades of mutation rate) while the 2-byte pusher does not; faithfulness at L = 100 switches on between 128 and 256 steps; at 64 steps the soup replicates heritably without clones; E7 is confirmed; seeds are exchangeable. Not settled: why 32-step soups never find the pusher although it is viable in isolation at L = 16; whether the sub-clonal 64-step regime is stable beyond 300k steps; the mechanism of the pusher's heritability dip at L = 9–12 (candidate: stack-pointer wrap on the 2L ring; testable with the Stage F ring-length variants at L = 10 and 12 without new shaders); the emptiness of L = 3, 4, 7 (discovery vs maintenance).
