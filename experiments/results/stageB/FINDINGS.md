# Stage B — organism size (L = 4 … 100): findings (rewritten 2026-10-07 after two reviews)

Every number below is copied from `NUMBERS.md` in this directory (generated by `findings.py` from `assays.csv`, `cells.csv`, `succession.csv` and the soup snapshots). Stage B is 640 runs on Modal L40S: L ∈ {4, 9, 25, 36, 49, 64, 81, 100} × {none, block-copy, stack-writes, no-copy} × {128, 512} Z80 steps × mutation 1/16 × seeds 1–10, horizon 300k steps, sampled every 500 steps, early stop 4 samples after quasispecies occupancy ≥ 50%. L = 16 comes from Stage A (same seeds, same settings).

**Status: exploratory.** The pre-registered primary outcome (`tq_10`, occupancy) fires on zero-byte floods as well as on replicators (no-copy L = 36 · 512 steps: 10/10 crossings, 0 replicators), so the conclusions use the assay outcomes adopted after 19 Stage A runs were read: `t_rep` (a top-3 exemplar with ≥ 0.5% share is heritable: gen2 ≥ 0.3) and `t_faith` (additionally ≥ 50% of its partners became ≥ 75% copies). Times are Kaplan–Meier medians censored at each run's last step; "NR" = the curve never reaches 0.5; 500 is the sampling floor. The 46-opcode arm called `stack-writes` removes the stack, exchange, call/return and RST instructions, 24 of which write nothing; Stage C/D split it. Confirmation comes from Stages C–E (PLAN.md), not from this file.

- Above 16 bytes the unablated and block-copy-ablated soups produce a heritable replicator in every seed at every L, faithful in every seed except 8/10 at L = 100 · 128 steps (one of the two remaining runs was stopped early by the occupancy rule before faithfulness could be seen). KM medians are 500–1,500 steps at 128 steps and 500 at 512 steps: **at 500-step resolution no growth of emergence time with L is detectable.** Whether a real sub-500-step effect exists is what Stage E's fine sampling and control arms test. Block-copy removal is indistinguishable from the unablated arm at every L ≥ 16.
- Below 16 bytes there is a floor: L = 4 gave 1 replicator in 80 runs; L = 9 is marginal and is the one size where the stack ablation reverses sign (§2).
- With the 46-opcode arm removed, replication is LDIR-based and 5–50× slower; at 128 steps and L ≥ 81 the LDIR replicators are **heritable but not faithful** (L = 81: 2 of 7; L = 100: 0 of 10, 3 of them cut by the early stop at 64k–211k steps while the unfaithful cloud saturated the soup, so the 0 is a lower bound). One LDIR iteration copies one byte per Z80 step, so a 128-step encounter cannot reproduce an 81–100-byte tape, and the partner receives a partial copy that still copies. At 512 steps faithfulness returns (9/9 and 10/10). This is the "steps per byte" confound the review flagged, visible in the data.
- no-copy (112 instructions removed): 0 heritable replicators in 160 runs at any L (0/90 at L = 16 in Stage A; rmw-only 0/90).

## 2. L = 9: removing the stack arm raises emergence (post hoc; one cell; confirmatory run = Stage D)

| budget | outcome | stack-writes | none | Fisher two-sided | one-sided (stack-writes greater) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 128 | t_rep | 9/10 (KM 92,000) | 4/10 (NR) | 0.057 | 0.029 |
| 512 | t_rep | 10/10 (KM 91,500) | 1/10 (NR) | 1.2 × 10⁻⁴ | 6 × 10⁻⁵ |
| 128 | tq_10 (pre-registered) | 8/10 | 10/10 | 0.47 | — |

- By the pre-registered occupancy event the contrast points the other way at 128 steps, because `tq_10` fires on the all-zero dominant tape of the unablated soups (zero-byte fraction of the final soup: mean 0.22, range 0.01–0.34 at 128 steps; 0.30 at 512; byte entropy 5.7–5.8 bits). With the arm removed the soups are not flooded (0.06; entropy 4.4–5.1 bits because an LDIR cloud has taken over).
- The zero flood is **not** an L = 9 phenomenon: at the `tq_10` crossing, unablated soups are 0.24–0.34 zeros at 128 steps at every L from 9 to 100 (0.17–0.26 at 512 steps). Nor is it purely a stack phenomenon: a local run with only the 22 writing stack opcodes removed still reaches ≈ 0.19 zeros within 500 steps (loads of zero registers through LD (HL),r and LD (nn),rr, block copies of zeros), so the stack roughly doubles the flood rather than creating it. At L ≥ 16 a stack replicator rises out of the flood within 500–1,500 steps; at L = 9 none does, and the LDIR route is slow (KM ≈ 92k steps when the arm is removed) and mostly fails within 300k steps when it is present. Which of the 46 removed opcodes matters (PUSH/CALL/RST writers, or the POP/RET/EX pointer routes LDIR designs use), and whether zeros or sterile stack smears suppress LDIR, is what Stage D's arms separate.
- The replicator at L = 9 is LDIR-based in every run with one: period 3 (the 3-byte unit `DEC E ; LDIR`, DE = 0x00FF ≡ 3 mod 18) in 7 and 8 of the stack-writes runs at 128 and 512 steps, 9 in 2 and 2; the four unablated successes are 3, 3, 9, 9.
- n = 10 per arm; the 128-step contrast survives no multiplicity correction, the 512-step one survives Bonferroni over the 64 Stage B cells. Stage D re-tests it with 20 new seeds per arm and pre-stated one-sided tests.

## 3. Large organisms are mostly tiled; whole-tape copiers exist but stay rare

- The first heritable replicator of every unablated and block-copy run at L = 25, 36, 49, 64 and 81 has **period 2** (10/10 at each L and budget; 8/10 at L = 100 · 128 steps, the other two periods 8 and 20): the Load–Push pair `LD rr,nn ; PUSH rr` (`01 c5`) or `LD HL,nn ; EX (SP),HL` (`21 e3`) repeated across the tape. A 100-byte organism is fifty copies of a 2-byte instruction pair. Note 2 does not divide the odd lengths 25, 49, 81; it divides the 2L-byte pair memory, the ring the copy runs on.
- With the stack arm removed the first replicators are LDIR shift-copies tiled at periods dividing 2L in every tiled case at L = 9, 16, 25, 36, 49, 64, 81 and in 90% at L = 100 (3 | 18; 4, 8, 16 | 32; 5 | 50; 4, 6, 9, 12 | 72; 7 | 98; 8, 16, 32 | 128; 9, 27 | 162; 8, 10, 20, 25 | 200). Mechanism (verified with the executor): copying a tape onto itself at offset d on a ring of 2L bytes converges to period gcd(d, 2L), and an offset with gcd 1 scrambles the parent. The copy offset is now recorded by the assay so the gcd law is tested directly in Stages C–E.
- **Whole-tape copiers do exist.** Among the final faithful exemplars, 6 tapes at L = 49, 64 and 81 (5 under the stack ablation, 1 unablated) have period L and copy their partner exactly: aperiodic LDIR/LDDR designs carrying 40–75 bytes of high-entropy cargo, at 0.06–0.2% share. Three Stage B runs (stack-writes, L = 49 and 81) even have a whole-tape copier as their *first* replicator. So the budget allows them and selection does not forbid them; they simply do not out-compete the tiled designs. My earlier statement that no whole-tape replicator above 32 bytes appeared was wrong.
- Final-soup high-order entropy (median over seeds) is 0.6–2.6 bits/byte under the stack family at L ≥ 25 and 3.4–6.0 under LDIR (NUMBERS.md §4); the functional fraction of random final cells (random partners) is 0.14–0.88 under the stack family and 0.86–1.00 under LDIR.
- **H6 (larger organisms → more complex emergence) is not supported** by this stage: the same two mechanism classes at every L ≥ 16, more repeats rather than more information, with rare whole-tape copiers as the exception. The size axis has four confounds (per-byte mutation ∝ 1/L; windows and bytes ∝ L; steps per byte ∝ 1/L; parity-driven early stop) that Stage E was designed to remove; until then "tiling, not complexity" is an observation, not a result.

## 4. Succession (census family at 300k steps, or at the last step for runs that stopped early)

- Unablated, 128 steps: the stack family takes over (≥ 30% headroom-normalised excess) in 10/10 seeds at L = 25–64 with median takeover 1,500–2,000 steps, 9/10 at L = 81, 6/10 at L = 100. LDIR holds the soup at the end in 1/10 seeds at L = 49 and 81 and 5/10 at L = 100; at L = 81 and 100 these are LDIR-first runs (takeover at 1,500–3,000 steps), not invasions of a stack soup. Late invasions (73k–263k steps) occur at L = 25 and 36 at 512 steps and L = 49 at 128 steps.
- The early stop fired in 0–10 of 10 runs per cell and tracks tape parity in most cells: even L (36, 64, 100) phase-lock on one period-2 phase and stop at 2.5–5k steps; odd L (25, 49) split into two phase variants at Hamming distance L and run to 300k (L = 81 · 512 stopped in 4/10). Final-state comparisons across L therefore mix soups of different ages in this stage; Stage C5/E run without the stop.
- Stage A at L = 16: unablated soups end in LDIR in 23/30 seeds at 32 steps (LDIR takeover median 68k steps after a stack takeover at 12k), and in a stack family in 14 and 26 of 30 at 128 and 512 steps.

## 5. Measurement results carried into the paper

- Assay null: 200 random 16-byte tapes give gen2 ≤ 0.03; the worst non-replicator (an all-NOP tape, credited for its partners' pushed zeros) reaches 0.13; threshold 0.3. A drifting `CALL` chain in the no-copy L = 100 soups passes gen2 (0.49) but converts no partner into a ≥ 75% copy and dies by generation 3; the RST return-address smear converts 64% of partners but has gen2 0.09. Hence "faithful = gen2 ≥ 0.3 and ≥ 50% of partners became copies" throughout; each single criterion admits a known non-replicator.
- `t_rep` requires an exact genotype with ≥ 0.5% share, which maximally diverse LDIR clouds never have: Stage A stack-writes at 1/4 mutation shows 3–4/10 by `t_rep` but 10/10 by the final random-cell assay. The census takeover and the random-cell functional fraction are the measures for clouds.
- Pattern-based life detectors against functional ground truth over all 1,270 final soups (alive = ≥ 25% of random cells heritable): high-order entropy, the Computational Life indicator, has AUC 1.00 at L = 16 and 0.53 at L ≥ 25 (zero floods are structured and dead; odd-L stack soups are alive and unstructured); unique-tape fraction 0.98, species entropy 0.97, brotli bits/byte 0.97, top exact share 0.82, quasispecies share 0.74, zero-flood census 0.37.

## 6. Open confounds (REVIEW.md §4) and what resolves them
Per-byte mutation ∝ 1/L and steps per byte ∝ 1/L (Stage E @mubyte, @steps8L, @budget); lottery size ∝ L (@bytes); parity (@nominal without stop, non-square L); the 500-step resolution and the unobserved zero-flood transient (fine and explicit early sampling in C–E); the 46-opcode arm mixing writers with pointer routes (C3 and D arms); within-seed GPU non-determinism as a share of between-seed variance (E6 and the declared L = 16 cross-batch replicates).
