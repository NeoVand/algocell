The new manuscript follows `planning/narrative/JOINT_CHARTER.md` and the user’s subsequent decisions. It uses `STYLE_NOTES.md` and `REWRITE_PLAN.md` for prose guidance; the latter’s older universal open-to-closed narrative is superseded. `MAIN_nature.md`, `MAIN.md`, `SI.md`, figures, registrations and source tables were not edited. No experiments were run for this rewrite.

## The argument was rebuilt around retention

The old title and abstract organized the paper as replication, individuality and then heredity. The new draft offers three title candidates, uses a neutral working title, and develops a mechanism-to-lineage-to-ecology argument: reproduction can erase introduced differences; forward overlap identifies which differences survive; the copying displacement can itself be inherited; environments change the prevalence of the resulting designs.

The introduction now motivates the problem before describing the apparatus. It credits the established distinction between reproduction and heredity, including von Neumann and the autocatalytic-system literature. The claim is the inspectable evolved mechanism and its intervention predictions, not priority for recognizing that reproduction alone is insufficient for adaptive evolution.

Woese’s Darwinian threshold remains a framing in the abstract, introduction and discussion. Closure now means zero partner-to-offspring inflow under a declared context distribution. Instruction-fetch confinement is a separate observable. The text makes clear that Woese’s biological account did not require horizontal transfer to vanish. Heredity of variation is a distinct threshold; neither a universal chronology nor a universal necessity relation is asserted.
