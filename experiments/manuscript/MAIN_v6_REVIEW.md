12 October 2026. Reviewed every factual sentence of the abstract and main text (lines 17–300), against the cited archives, scorers, registration, current Zilion records, primary literature, v5 and `STYLE_NOTES.md`. Repeated instances of the same problem are grouped below. Line numbers refer to the reviewed v6, SHA-256 `a7020d369c7603cb1b8fcd8ac8dd4f737f06996284a4c36d84cd60b99cff3cf7`. Methods, legends and the ED/SI map are outside the editing scope; they are identical to v5 and were consulted as definitions, not treated as independent evidence. Relative source links below resolve from this review's directory.

Its abstract and introduction climb the requested ladders more clearly; the new inherited-rule section gives the paper a real centre. The voice is an improvement. The 46 bullet lines, generic headings and fragmented discussion are not: v5 better matched continuous claim–evidence–interpretation prose and the four-part discussion.

Most headline numbers survive scrutiny: 48/64, the 512-type pusher16 endpoint, 38%, nine sites, the rare 5/2,048 pusher64 types, 2,347/15,655, 2,342/2,343, all 53 excess cases, the 99/50 allele counts, the L=32 competition medians, C1's 8/10, and the 6502's 20/20, 14/20, 1,125,000 and 296/296. They do not need rhetorical weakening; they need their actual units, contracts and status. The eight-transfer offset-marker evidence is real, and the inherited-trait sentence is worth saving in its payload-specific form. The coherent-fetch 8086 is an explicitly declared abstraction with substantial validation; `B9 51` has supporting encounter tests, although v6 reverses its immediate value, and the REP law test remains a conditional construction and prospective assay.

The changes needed before accepting this narrative pass are substantive: remove invented priority, distinguish C1 reruns from historical results, replace equilibrium and no-open-phase assertions with the observed endpoints, restore the fixed-core and marker-versus-capacity distinctions, and keep Woese an explicit analogue. The charter's clearest forbidden returns are “inherited nothing” and the regenerator/transmitter zero-heredity dichotomy; the tidy environmental ladder also approaches the forbidden universal chronology. V6 does **not** claim selection for evolvability—the explicit caveat is sound—and should not be criticised as though it did. Preserve the character; restore the distinctions that make the claims true.
