# Resolution of the MAIN_v7 numerical audit

This completed ledger resolves the **153 unresolved/not-found occurrences and the one mismatch** in `audit_v7.csv`: **154 distinct original row IDs**, exactly once each. The 528 previously verified occurrences are outside this exception ledger. Original locations refer to `MAIN_v7.md`; wording moved from its long legends belongs in v8 Methods or Supplementary Information, with the same source and scope. No row is resolved merely by deleting it or by finding the same numeral elsewhere.

Paths below are relative to `experiments/`. **Source** means the number or condition is established by the named generated field, generated machine contract, or its exact registered/implementation definition; definitions and plotting constants are not empirical measurements. **Derivation** gives the arithmetic or explicit set cardinality to state in Methods. **Correction** changes the substantive claim. The per-occurrence entries supply the finer provenance that a nearest-bracket string matcher cannot infer. All retained result-path brackets in the review copy refer to this ledger when a figure-only field or a protocol constant needs a second source. Numerical equality never certifies ancestry or theorem eligibility.

The substantive correction is N0046: **six total scored sites, four unexecuted**, at period four and displacement eight. The motif scan also requires a denominator clarification: **11,700 scheduled top-three sample records comprise 35,100 ranked tape entries**, not 11,700 individual tapes; the separate checkpoint denominator is **2,000,000 cells**. This clarification preserves the observed zero motif counts.
