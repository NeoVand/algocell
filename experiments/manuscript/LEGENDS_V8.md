Colours are defined separately in each panel. Byte-grid entries are hexadecimal. Detailed protocols and numerical provenance are in Methods and Supplementary Notes 4–5.

**Figure 1. Reproduction made inspectable.**

**a,** The schematic shows two Z80 copying mechanisms. The pusher uses the same bytes as instructions and literal output: `01 c5 01` loads BC, and `c5` pushes it into partner memory. Execution can enter the partner; a separate return-containing design redirects execution into its own tape. These are not a documented ancestor–descendant pair. Ellipses abbreviate tapes; `e3` exchanges HL with stack memory. **b,** Cumulative detection is one minus Kaplan–Meier survival for historical Stage E worlds at six tape lengths L (bytes), with 10 worlds per length and nondetections censored at 300,000 updates. Encounters have a 128-instruction budget. Operational heritability requires generation-two normalized culture gain ≥ 0.3. Updates are encounter batches. **c,** One illustrative L = 16 world, seed 2002, contains 20,000 cells. Curves show cells matching the contemporaneous modal tape at ≥ 12/16 unshifted positions, zero bytes/all bytes and distinct tapes/all cells. Similarity is not a reproduction assay. **d,** Five validation comparisons use distinct denominators: hardware-recorded 8088 instruction states; historical versus corrected Z80 ring conditions; isolated assay combinations; claim verdicts and constituent tests. The ring criterion is a 95% upper bound on changed-memory rate below 0.01%. Ochre marks expected hardware divergences; vermilion marks flagged or changed comparisons; grey marks agreement under the row-specific criterion. The 10,083 hardware divergences are expected; none is unexpected. Queue timing is outside the contract. Core C1 denotes corrected Z80 semantics; rows must not be pooled as one accuracy estimate.
