# v6 layout, frozen before plotting

All composed figures: 180 mm wide, white, Helvetica-like sans; 6.5 pt body/ticks,
8 pt bold lowercase letters, aligned by row. No tight-bbox resizing. Teal and
vermilion encode retained versus erased variation; grey encodes loss/structure.
Figure 4 uses teal for benign and house purple for lethal chemistry; historical
is dashed/open and C1 is solid/filled. Labels occupy reserved header/footer bands.
