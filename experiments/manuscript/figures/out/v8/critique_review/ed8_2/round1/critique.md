The main problem is not a universal font-size threshold. At 180 mm width, much of the text appears broadly comparable to the references’ small body text. The greater weaknesses are cryptic identifiers, an incorrect colour key, and missing labels that cannot be replaced by a separate methods legend.

**Substantive defects**

**a — All historical serial designs**

- **The key contradicts the plotted colours.** It says “red alive <50%,” but the intermediate segments appear light grey, not red. Change the wording and add actual colour swatches matching all three fills.
- **The bars lack a named quantitative measure.** There is no axis identifying what population is partitioned, or explicitly establishing that each bar totals one. Add a compact fraction axis, such as “Share of [scored population] at transfer 8,” with 0, 0.5 and 1 ticks.
- **The 50% carriage threshold has no defined base.** “Alive ≥50% carriage” does not identify what is counted to calculate carriage. A short, explicit legend label must establish that meaning; the full procedure can remain outside the figure.
- **“Ctrl.” and “Sites” are insufficiently defined.** The decimal control values do not identify the control outcome, while the site counts do not identify the qualifying condition. Replace these headers with descriptive labels and supply the control denominator or a shared sample-size note where applicable.
- **The design list lacks the scientific category headers used effectively in the references.** Labels such as `ret16`, `ldir20`, `jr50` and `cl.32_p1` require an external crosswalk. Add category headers and readable design names, retaining archive identifiers secondarily. Explicitly identify genome length if it is needed to interpret the comparisons.

**b — Complete branching maps**

- **The stacked bars have no local or explicitly shared key or fraction axis.** Their meaning depends on borrowing the already problematic key in a. Use one clearly shared legend for a–b, or repeat a compact key, and identify the population partitioned by these bars.
- **The numerical columns remain ambiguous.** “48/64” provides a denominator, but neither the numerator event nor the counted unit is named. “Sites” likewise lacks its scoring endpoint. Label the control outcome, what the 64 units represent, and the site criterion.

A short scope label identifying these as the displayed archived design–partner combinations would resolve this without requiring additional results.

**c — Complete serial site maps**

- **The maps have no position scale or explicit length labels.** All rows occupy the same width despite visibly different cell subdivisions. Readers cannot readily identify site numbers or determine how position is normalized between designs. Label genome length per row and add usable site-position landmarks, or explicitly state that each row spans its full genome.

**d — Partner-environment carriage tests**

The numerical annotations already communicate the heatmap values effectively; a separate colour bar is not essential if the measure and scale are clearly labelled.
