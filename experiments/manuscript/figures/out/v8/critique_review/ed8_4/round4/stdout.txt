**Substantive defects**

Exact font sizes cannot be established from the PNG, but the apparent print scale is inadequate. Panels **c, e, and f** are particularly affected. Enlarge all text to at least 6 pt and substantive labels to at least 6.5 pt. Recover space from the large title-to-content gaps and redistribute panel heights while preserving the dense information.

- **Panel a — the two nonzero trajectories are too faint:** These pale cyan lines carry the principal exception to the zero median, yet have insufficient contrast for reliable reading at print size. Darken them and modestly increase their weight. Retain the useful “18 zero trajectories overlap median” annotation; place “2/20 worlds majority confined” nearer the upper trajectories.

- **Panel a — the horizontal unit is inconsistent:** The axis says “World updates (millions),” but the result annotation switches to “at 3M.” Replace this with “at 3 million updates” or “at final update,” making the endpoint explicit without requiring interpretation.

Expand the first to “Confined rows” and provide a compact local key for T. Keep the existing statement that these are pooled sampled-cell rows and that the classes are subsets.
