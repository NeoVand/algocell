**Substantive defects and concrete fixes**

- **Panel a — excessive space weakens the presentation.** The large gaps between title, legend, first category, and subsequent categories make four observations occupy an unusually large area. They look underfilled beside the dense panel c. Tighten these gaps and use the recovered space to enlarge text and panel d. Preserve the informative category headers and numeric columns.

- **Panel a — the measurement and denominator are ambiguous.** The title describes “branching lineages,” while the axis measures “all starting mutant populations.” The legend does not establish whether “Alive, carried” means any surviving descendant carries the allele or some other endpoint criterion. State the counted unit and the rule for assigning each starting population to a bar segment. Identify whether the “Unmutated alive” column is a separate control and whether it uses the same eight-generation endpoint.

- **Panel a — “Scored sites” does not explain what is counted.** It could mean sites tested, sites meeting a transmission criterion, or sites carrying alleles at the endpoint. The block-copy row has a visible teal sliver but reports 0/32, which is difficult to interpret without the criterion. Define the numerator and denominator, and explain how a nonzero carried fraction can coexist with zero qualifying sites.

- **Panels a and c — apparently matching specimens have unexplained differences.** “Evolved genome, L = 20” has 9/20 scored sites in a, whereas c reports 12 sites at transfer 8. These need not conflict, but the image does not explain whether this reflects different assays, criteria, or genomes. Give the specimen a consistent identifier and explicitly distinguish the two measurements.

- **Panels a and b — the provenance notes are not self-contained.** “Historical = C1,” “Pusher X1-2 prediction failed,” and “Historical” require outside knowledge. Neither C1 nor X1-2 is explained, and the failed prediction is not identified. Replace these with a plain-language explanation of what was compared and what failed, or move internal identifiers out of the figure while retaining the substantive finding.

- **Panel b — individual curves cannot be identified.** Several transmitter and pusher trajectories share colour without names, lengths, or identifiers. The category labels explain the broad separation but cannot connect individual curves to the examples in a or c. Add compact identifiers at curve endpoints or a nearby mapping key, including a clear indication of coincident curves where applicable.

State what each curve represents and whether its values pool lineages across mutation sites or average site-specific fractions. Define the plotted denominator.

- **Panel c — the last numeric column has an ambiguous referent.** “Of these, unexecuted” presumably refers to sites carried at transfer 8, but that relationship should be explicit. Use “Transfer-8 sites never executed.” Also specify the observation period for “Byte executed” and “Never executed”; this matters to the biological interpretation of transmission without execution.

Add a compact before/after state or source-to-destination copying schematic, explicitly identifying the mutation at position 8 and the overwriting event at step 6.
