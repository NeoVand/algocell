## Abstract

Reproduction supplies evolution with descendants, but adaptation also requires descendants to preserve differences. How a spontaneously arising copier acquires that ability remains a mechanistic question. Here we introduce Zilion, an inspectable execution substrate, and combine digital-population experiments with interventions on isolated reproductive lineages. Registered branching transfers show that lineages can survive while losing the tested variation. In a recurring copying architecture, forward overlap replaces the tape with repetitions of a protected prefix; an inherited displacement controls how much freely variable payload that prefix contains. Exploratory allele interventions support this control while exposing the limits of thresholded heredity scores. Environmental interventions alter the prevalence of regenerating and transmitting designs, without establishing selection for future adaptability. Closure to partner information and heredity of variation are therefore distinct achievements, providing a restricted experimental framing of Woese’s Darwinian threshold. Comparative results show different routes into replication; a prospective 8086 test of route and copying geometry remains pending.

## Main text

### What reproduction passes on

A system can make more of itself without providing evolution with much to change. A reproductive process may repeatedly restore a preferred pattern, removing differences introduced into its parents. Such a system can persist, spread and withstand perturbation, yet fail to preserve the particular variation on which further adaptation would depend. The distinction between reproduction and the inheritance of alternatives is established in theories of self-reproducing automata and limited heredity, and in studies of autocatalytic systems. What requires explanation is how a reproductive mechanism admits some differences and removes others. [von Neumann, *Theory of Self-Reproducing Automata*](https://fab.cba.mit.edu/classes/862.16/notes/computation/vonNeumann-1966.pdf); [Vasas, Szathmáry and Santos](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2824406/); [Vasas et al., *Evolution before genes*](https://doi.org/10.1186/1745-6150-7-1).

Woese’s Darwinian threshold offers a useful framing, provided its scope is kept clear. He described the emergence of integrated cellular organizations and stable genealogies from an evolutionary regime dominated by horizontal exchange. Here we distinguish closure, meaning that partners contribute no information to an offspring under a specified encounter distribution, from heredity of variation, meaning that introduced parental differences survive reproduction. Zero inflow is our deliberately strict operational analogue of the horizontal-to-vertical step; Woese did not require biological horizontal transfer to disappear. Neither threshold is a universal law of evolutionary succession. [Woese, *On the evolution of cells*](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC124369/).

Random-program soups make these distinctions experimentally accessible. Earlier work established spontaneous digital replication, compact copying cores and succession between reproductive architectures. We build on those discoveries by asking what the descendants retain. Abundance cannot answer that question: a successful copier may erase variation precisely because it restores its pattern so effectively. Nor does fidelity to an unmodified parent establish fidelity to its mutants. The informative intervention changes a parent, allows reproduction to continue, and follows the change separately from the lineage. [Agüera y Arcas et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.19108v1); [Cicala et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.09211v2).

We introduce Zilion to expose those operations. Our central result connects a copying geometry to the parental coordinates that remain distinguishable in descendants. An inherited displacement can change that geometry, while environmental conditions change the prevalence of the resulting designs. An account of the origin of replication is incomplete until it explains the inheritance of differences.

### Making reproduction inspectable

Zilion separates a processor’s instruction semantics from the world in which programs encounter one another. Its versioned contract specifies memory addressing, initial machine state, execution resources, halting and observation. This separation makes a reproductive claim portable enough to test: the same parental tape can be examined against different partners, its copying addresses traced, and its descendants challenged with new partners (Fig. 1). Zilion is introduced here as an experimental instrument. The historical populations were produced by Algocell kernels; later C1 measurements and Zilion implementations retain their own identities rather than being retrospectively assigned a common executable.

An encounter concatenates a focal tape and a partner, initializes the processor, executes a bounded program and writes both resulting tapes back. The population supplies encounters and background mutation, without a fitness score or externally imposed replacement by successful genotypes. Reproductive success occurs when execution writes a form capable of further reproduction. Cell boundaries, processing time, reset state and partner access are supplied resources. The code that uses them to reproduce evolves within that contract. This distinction matters because an evolved copier may depend on a register supplied at reset just as strongly as on an instruction encoded in its tape.

We keep persistence, confinement, partner dependence and retention separate. A culture assay tests whether a tape makes descendants that copy in turn. Tracing measures confinement of instruction-stream fetches to the focal half of memory. With the parent fixed, variation among offspring across partners measures inflow from those partners. Mutation and transfer assays ask whether a particular parental difference persists. Confinement does not establish zero inflow, because a program can read partner data without fetching partner instructions; open execution can also have zero inflow if the partner has already been overwritten. The entropy identity behind this distinction is given in Methods.

The elementary examples make the need for these measurements concrete. A pusher loads instruction-stream bytes and pushes them into memory, then runs into its surroundings. A regenerator repeatedly rebuilds a pattern from part of its tape. A transmitter preserves a larger set of introduced differences. These are experimentally resolved mechanisms and operational classes, not successive levels of life. Their behavior must be measured in the encounter conditions in which the claim is made. Instrument validation accordingly extends from instructions to ring encounters, isolated reproduction and population outcomes; success at one level does not certify the others.

### Lineages survive while alleles vanish

Serial transfer initially leaves an ambiguity: a mutation can disappear because its lineage dies. The registered branching experiment X1 addressed this ambiguity by allowing each surviving member to generate multiple descendants. Under uniform fresh partners, 48 of 64 unmutated pusher16 populations survived through generation eight, while none of the 512 tested single mutants retained its marker in any of its replicate populations at that endpoint (Fig. 2). Mutant populations also survived with their markers erased: disappearance was not confined to mutant lineages that died. The survival component X1-1 was met; the predicted retention component X1-2 failed. That failure is the result, not a successful prediction of erasure. [results/branching/branching_summary.csv, key=pusher16, arm=U, gen=8, ctrl_alive_n/ctrl, n_mutants and mut_alive/erased/retained; results/branching/SUPP_TABLE.md; results/tier2/T2_COMPONENTS.csv, X1-1/X1-2]

The controls expose distinct reasons for losing a difference. The return regenerator and block-copy tiling preserved their unmutated populations but had no scored transmissible sites at the final branching endpoint. The evolved transmitter retained nine scored sites under the same protocol. A site score requires many alternative values to pass a specified retention threshold; it is stronger than finding an occasional surviving allele and weaker than proving transmission of every possible payload. This distinction prevents a population’s reproductive success from being mistaken for a general hereditary channel. [results/branching/branching_summary.csv, ret16, ldir32 and genome20_s6003, arm=U, gen=8; results/branching/SUPP_TABLE.md]

Zero scored sites does not mean zero hereditary information. Pusher64 retained some tested mutant types even though no position met the site threshold: 5 of 2,048 types with uniform partners and 18 of 2,048 with open-phase soup partners. The block-copy regenerator likewise retained rare core variants. These observations rule out describing all regenerators as unique sequence attractors or claiming that nothing whatsoever can be inherited. What is absent in these assays is broad tolerance of alternative values at scored positions. [results/branching/SUPP_TABLE.md, pusher64 and block-copy tiling rows]

Partner choice also changes survival. The parent-by-environment experiment distinguishes loss of a copying lineage from survival with erasure, including failures under altered register resets. A tape that cannot reproduce in a particular environment has not thereby been shown to lack payload capacity in environments where its copying mechanism works. Conversely, survival with an erased allele demonstrates that extinction is not needed to remove variation. Soup partners create an additional problem: partners that already resemble the parent can inflate apparent survival. We therefore anchor the branching contrast in the uniform-partner arm and report the soup arm with its background and reacquisition limitations. [results/pe/pe_matrix.csv; results/branching/BRANCHING.md]

An exploratory provenance analysis helps localize copying and erasure, but does not quantitatively explain X1. Composing the wild-type byte-response map across generations omits branching, changing contexts and the assay’s window and viability conditions; excluding opcode destinations does not repair the forecast. A byte may affect a copied operand, change future execution, or survive without its surrounding marker. We retain these failed local forecasts because they identify what a mechanistic explanation must supply: a causal route into descendants, a viable family in which that route repeats, and an explicit match to the assay being predicted. [results/crystal/prediction_comparison.csv; results/crystal/site_prediction_comparison.csv; results/crystal/CRYSTAL.md, exploratory H1]

### Overlap determines the payload

Forward copying provides such an explanation in a recurring family. Imagine moving bytes along a ring, each write a fixed displacement ahead of its read. The earliest reads obtain original parental bytes. Once the read pointer catches the region already written, it reads copies of those earlier bytes. Repetition therefore propagates a prefix around the ring. A small displacement restores a short pattern even when the original tape contains other material; a displacement spanning the parent can preserve its entire sequence. The mechanism explains erasure by identifying the overwrite that causes it (Fig. 3a).

For a qualifying forward copy with displacement $d$, protected core footprint $c$, $c\le d\le L$, $d\mid L$, sufficient coverage and aligned offspring entry, the independent freely variable payload has size

\[
r=d-c.
\]

This is a theorem about a specified copying operation with its core fixed. The first $d$ source coordinates supply the final ring; $c$ constrained core coordinates lie inside that prefix, leaving the remaining coordinates independently variable. Later coordinates are overwritten before they can contribute independently. The proof requires protection of the prefix, the correct copy direction and address progression, preservation of the executable core, and an offspring in the same admissible family. It is not a formula for every copier or every thresholded site score.

Consider a change in the protected payload and another beyond the copying prefix. The former reaches the advancing write pointer while it is still an original parental value, then spreads through every output position assigned to that source. The latter is replaced before the read pointer reaches it. Both parents can produce viable descendants, but only the former difference remains available to distinguish their lineages. Repeated copies of a source do not multiply the number of independent choices: they are several manifestations of the same inherited coordinate. Increasing displacement can expose new original coordinates to copying, provided the altered core still executes correctly. The prediction concerns those coordinates, not a resemblance between the final tape and a repeated pattern.

The Z80 forms `XX 5e ed b0` and `1e NN ed b0` make the operation tangible. The former executes its initial byte and then reads it as the value controlling displacement; the latter encodes the displacement as an immediate operand. Under the initialization, coverage and divisibility hypotheses in Methods, their fixed core occupies four bytes, giving $r=d-4$. The family recurs in 2,347 of 15,655 sampled confined heritable cells. It is an experimentally useful architecture, not the whole ecology. Cicala and colleagues previously identified the core and annotated its partner offset; the new claim concerns its consequences for persistent variation. [results/offset/CORE_FAMILIES.md, generated core-family census; results/offset/offset_cells.csv; theorem, Methods; Cicala et al., cited above]

Under these hypotheses, every final coordinate comes from the parental prefix, whatever the partner initially held. The mechanism therefore establishes closure while allowing extensive erasure of parental differences. Closure removes an external source of variation; payload preservation retains an internal one. Their separation is already present in the copying operation, before any population-frequency comparison is made. Conversely, a working core variant can persist even when the fixed-core payload is empty. The thresholds concern different properties of reproduction and must be assayed with different interventions.

An exploratory reanalysis resolves a misleading comparison in the original population test. Among 2,343 cells with a dividing displacement in range, 2,342 had $d-4$ scored unexecuted positions, whereas 2,289 had that many total scored sites. All 53 excess-total cases had the predicted unexecuted count; their additional scores belonged to fetched positions. The remaining deficit concerns a parent whose projected offspring fails the whole-tape similarity gate despite preserving the targeted payload in the follow-up assay. These are sampled site measurements, not exhaustive certificates: motif recognition and a dividing offset alone do not establish every hypothesis of the theorem. [results/crystal/cells.csv, source=offset/offset_cells.csv; results/crystal/cell_exceptions.csv; results/crystal/offset_examples.csv; results/offset/offset_cells.csv]

The copying prefix also differs from the smallest repeated unit visible in an unmodified tape. An exploratory counterexample has minimal period four, displacement eight and four scored unexecuted positions. Its minimal unit contains only the core, yet variants can distinguish the larger prefix that reproduction preserves. Capacity therefore belongs to a family of possible sequences under a copying transformation, not to the visual periodicity of a single sequence. Minimal sequence periodicity cannot stand in for hereditary capacity. [results/crystal/cell_exceptions.csv, source_row=1064, K, L=32, seed=5006, final; results/crystal/offset_examples.csv, example=unit-cell expands under mutation]

The displacement itself can be inherited. Archived serial interventions changing ldir32’s initial byte to `08` or `60` retained the mutant marker through generation eight; under the qualifying geometry these settings change displacement to eight or thirty-two and predict four or twenty-eight free payload coordinates. The observations establish persistent working core variants; they do not independently measure the descendants’ complete copying kernel and expanded capacity. The reciprocal-edit, full-core and descendant-payload audit remains PENDING. [results/serial_retention/sr_mutants.csv.gz, key=ldir32, gen=8, pos=0, val=8/96; theorem, Methods] [fill: generated reciprocal-edit and descendant-capacity table]

The exploratory offset dial reinforces both the finding and its restriction. For ldir32, 99 of 256 settings passed the culture criterion and 50 working settings met the aligned byte-equality criterion. Among eleven working settings passing the sampled eligibility checks, displacements ranged from four to thirty-two, predicting payload from zero to twenty-eight. Aligned equality can be incidental or partner-sourced; it is not serial certification of every setting. The genome20 transmitter had 15 working settings, but uses reverse `LDDR` copying and lies outside the forward theorem. [results/crystal/alleles_ldir32.csv; results/crystal/alleles_genome20_s6003.csv; results/crystal/CRYSTAL.md, exploratory H4]

This is an evolved instance of a familiar dual role: reproductive instructions can both influence construction and be inherited. The offset is not arbitrary payload; changing it changes the operation, and many values fail. Yet its inheritance shows why execution dependence cannot be a universal obstruction to heredity. A restricted noninterference certificate can prove that payload values survive, while working changes to the machinery require their own causal analysis. The next test is precise: independently eligible copiers on another processor must preserve the predicted coordinates, regardless of how frequently evolution discovers them.

The parameter result also separates variation in a copying rule from variation within its payload. A working offset change moves a lineage between admissible copying families; a payload change explores alternatives within one family. The rule can therefore be heritable without being freely mutable, while the payload can be freely mutable without doing useful work. Conflating these roles would make the mechanistic claim either too strong—all changes of machinery must survive—or too weak—only inert material can be inherited. Their separation exposes a concrete route by which reproduction can alter the repertoire of differences available to later descendants.

### Environments sort reproductive designs

Payload capacity matters ecologically only if the designs carrying it persist. In matched Z80 competitions, the environment changed their relative abundance. In the C1 mutation-on, equal-start experiments at tape length thirty-two, the generated report gives final median transmitter shares of 0.05 under benign tar and 0.76 under lethal tar, rounded there to two decimal places. These are shares among core-bearing cells, not the whole population. All ten worlds ended above half transmitter share, meeting the registered cohort endpoint. Both invasion directions and absolute core abundance are retained in Fig. 4 so that conditional success cannot conceal loss of the family. [results/tier2/scored/E/REPORT.md, L=32, mut=on, start=mix50; results/tier2/scored/E/offset_switch.csv; results/tier2/T2_COMPONENTS.csv, E-L32L]

Here lethality means that fetching a zero opcode halts the encounter, changing the execution semantics of the environment’s abundant by-product. The C1 lethality dial ended with transmitter-majority populations in eight of ten fully lethal worlds, compared with six of ten in the historical run. Its registered confinement-timing criterion, historically called closure, now passes with one remaining reversal; it is not a strictly monotone law. Original failures and updated passes are both retained. Because historical soups are not exactly reproducible from their seeds, these changes are rerun outcomes under C1, not effects causally assigned to the core correction. [results/tier2/T2_DZ.csv; results/tier2/T2_COMPONENTS.csv, DZ2/DZ3; results/dial/dial_worlds.csv]

The environment changes which designs prevail without prescribing their order of appearance. In the exploratory Stage G reanalysis, regenerators were first detected before transmitters in 30 of 42 worlds with both; ties and reversed order remain in the denominator. These are sparse population detections, not birth dates or resolved ancestry. Benign worlds often remained regenerator-dominated. Under lethal tar, the first detected tapes were already confined repeating forms, with no threshold-class transmitters: five were strict regenerators and five intermediate by the site score. Later transmitter prevalence therefore cannot be read backward as transmitter birth. [results/ladder/population_groups.csv; results/ladder/population_worlds.csv; results/crystal/first_cells.csv, Z80 I, first replicator]

The equilibrium mechanism remains incomplete. Encounter censuses locate asymmetries in interactions with core-free intruders, but registered toxin, backup-core and isolated mutation-flow explanations failed. The seeded designs also differ in their tails, so their sorting does not isolate selection on displacement alone. Nor do existing ablations establish that an early pusher phase causally facilitates later closure: they alter other construction pathways, and the required matched closure measurements are absent. The supported ecological claim is environmental sorting of reproductive designs; explaining its balance requires a prediction that survives an independently held-out intervention. [results/offset/OFFSET_ENCOUNTER.md; results/tier2/scored/E/OFFSET_ENCOUNTER.md; REVISION_PREREG.md, N1/N2/N4/E-mech; results/ladder/LADDER.md, exploratory H-midwife]

Why the balance is difficult to explain follows from the same shared-memory mechanism. A tape is not only an executor that makes descendants; it is also material another executor can enter and rewrite. Changing what the tape preserves changes what an intruder encounters. An isolated comparison of copying success can therefore miss an ecological consequence, while a demographic asymmetry observed in an established mixture can depend on the mixture that produced it. The encounter data narrow the possibilities but do not yet close this feedback. A satisfactory mechanism must predict how the balance moves after a specified intervention, including changes in the abundance and composition of the core-free population.

### Different machines, different routes

Processor comparison separates the availability of a mechanism from its evolutionary arrival. The validated 6502 census found no nonzero exact zero-partner self-writer at periods one through four in its declared length-sixteen arms. This finite negative result leaves near-copiers, longer writers and context-dependent copying available. Constructed forward-copy loops nevertheless reproduced the predicted $d-6$ payload in all 296 qualifying rows. They establish a realization of the copying geometry on another instruction set, without showing that evolution found those loops. [results/census6502/table_main.csv; results/census6502/table_closers_law.csv]

The registered population experiment S1 had a mixed outcome. All twenty first detected replicators in primary arm A were confined, but only fourteen met its registered numeric-program-counter-decrease criterion. P1 remains not supported. Ring-position analysis explains the remaining cases after inspection and does not replace the endpoint. P2 and the slower-arrival prediction P4 were supported; the median detection time was 1,125,000 steps. The evolved-offset component of P3 is untested because there were no eligible measurements. Final regenerator and transmitter classifications do not supply the missing retention assay on first replicators. [results/s6502/v2/S1_v2.md; results/s6502/CORRECTION.md; results/ladder/s6502_groups.csv; results/s6502/v2/verdict_comparison.csv, v2_frozen32; results/s6502/v2/arm_comparison.csv, protocol=v2_frozen32, arm=A]

BFF supplies a different boundary to the story. Its altered literal and halting rules dissociate open fetches from partner dependence: a writer can enter partner addresses after replacing their contents. The bandwidth and halting experiments therefore prevent us from making confinement a universal prerequisite of closure. They also prevent a universal pusher-to-loop succession from being inferred from the Z80 histories. The literal-channel results in Methods constrain particular writing grammars; they do not turn census availability into an arrival theorem. [results/bff/runs.csv; results/bff_dials/d2_bandwidth.csv; results/biology/individuality/per_replicator.csv, BFF rows]

The 8086 comparison is explicitly prospective (Fig. 5). Its selected abstraction uses coherent fetch: consumed instruction bytes come from current memory, without an 8088 prefetch queue. Instruction-level testing now records 3,137,000 hardware-derived cases with zero unexpected divergences under the declared rules; this is neither a completed encounter census nor certification of physical queue behavior. Literal writers, memory-fed open copiers and confined loops receive distinct classifications. ### From persistence to adaptive use

The two thresholds answer different evolutionary questions. Closure asks whether reproduction escapes dependence on the current partner. Heredity asks which differences the reproductive operation lets descendants carry forward. Many benign-world copiers achieve the former while repeatedly restoring a narrow pattern; harsher conditions favor designs that preserve more variation, although their first detected replicators need not already be transmitters. This gives Woese’s framing experimental content without asserting a universal chronology or equating digital closure with biological individuality.

The biological reach lies in this experimental distinction. Repeated recovery of the same composition could indicate faithful transmission, selective reconstruction or continual replacement from the environment. Following a deliberately introduced difference, together with the survival of its carriers and the source of the recovered material, separates those possibilities. The digital mechanism supplies a fully inspectable case, not a claim that chemistry uses a displacement byte. Its relevance is the causal question it makes unavoidable: which alternatives can the reproductive process preserve, and which does its own operation remove?

The overlap result explains more than a correlation between unused bytes and retention. It identifies the source of each descendant coordinate, predicts where interventions survive, and shows how an inherited parameter changes the answer. Its scope is also productive. Bytes can be inherited while influencing execution, and copied-but-unfetched bytes can disappear. A transferable explanation must follow what reproduction does to alternatives, rather than classify a tape by its appearance or count the addresses it executes.

Free payload is a capacity for storing variation, not evidence that this variation has acquired useful function. A neutral tag would demonstrate persistent lineage information. A functional payload would require a further result: inherited differences must alter performance in a specified environment and be maintained through reproduction. The dual reading of a description offers a route to that test, because copied material could later become interpretable machinery, but the transition has not been demonstrated here. Environmental sorting likewise does not establish selection for future adaptability. What these experiments establish is a tractable connection between reproductive operations and the alternatives they preserve, making the next questions about adaptive use and ecological maintenance experimentally exact.

Architectural address reduction precedes mapping into the pair ring. ### The overlap-copy result

**Theorem; restricted forward-copy family.** On a ring of $P$ cells, perform sequential writes $M[s+d+k]\leftarrow M[s+k]$, with addresses reduced modulo $P$, $0<d<P$, and no interfering operations. For $k<d$, the source has not yet been written and supplies its initial value. Thereafter its latest write occurred $d$ steps earlier. Induction gives original source coordinate $s+(k\bmod d)$ for every copied byte. If $d\mid P$, every write supplies the correct residue of a $d$-periodic tiling. Coverage $K\ge P-d$ fills the remaining ring. With $P=2L$ and $d\mid L$, the partner begins at the same phase as the parent.

The Z80 specialization retains the audited hypotheses of Proposition 8. For `XX 5e ed b0`, $L\le64$, $d=XX\bmod2L$, $4\le d\le L$, and $d\mid L$. The initial one-byte instruction must pass control to address 1, write only at addresses $d$ through $2L-1$, and leave $HL\equiv0$, $256D\equiv0\pmod{2L}$, with $BC=0$ or $BC\ge126$. Address increments must preserve the assumed displacement through the relevant work. These are theorem and contract constants, not population estimates. [manuscript/MAIN_nature.md, audited Proposition 8] [fill: generated proof-contract parameter table]

The unchanged initial byte is then loaded into E, establishing the displacement. Under the nominal budget, 126 block-copy executions remain; their coverage suffices at the stated lengths. The copied core is unchanged when revisited, so execution remains in the block move. The immediate form `1e NN ed b0` supplies the offset directly and leaves 127 block-copy executions. [theorem arithmetic under the same proof contract] Prefix positions outside the core can vary jointly without changing these conditions. The offspring retains that prefix and the same core, so induction across encounters proves serial transmission. Parental positions beyond the prefix are overwritten before supplying independent final information.

Thus, for constrained footprint $c$ wholly inside the protected prefix, the invariant product family has $r=d-c$ free coordinates, $256^r$ possible sequences and $8r$ ideal noiseless payload bits. This is cardinality arithmetic, not measured Shannon capacity, ecological neutrality or unlimited heredity. Working core alleles are additional possibilities outside this fixed-core count. A taken return can divert execution, a store can destroy the offset before it is read, insufficient coverage can leave partner bytes, and nondividing displacement can change the offspring phase or corrupt the core. Reverse `LDDR` designs require another derivation. The population motif census supplies candidates, not automatic eligibility certificates.

### Literal channels and their limits

**Theorem; repeated load–push pair.** Let distinct one-byte opcodes load $w$ literal bytes into a shared register and push a fixed permutation of them without altering that register. The stack pointer is separate and advances by $-w$; writes form contiguous blocks with no later interference. Assume the executed stream remains the prescribed repetition of load, literal and push. Execution and output periods are $w+2$ and $w$. A finite matching window permits the Fine–Wilf conclusion only when

\[
N\ge2w+2-\gcd(2,w).
\]

At this corrected bound, odd widths force a constant stream, contradicting distinct opcodes; even widths permit the alternating construction with a compatible output permutation. Ascending memory reverses the order of descending stack blocks, not their internal byte layout. Placement, coverage, phase and bootstrap are separate encounter requirements. [results/literal_channel/CHECK.md, corrected proof; results/literal_channel/pair_layouts.csv]

**Theorem; width-one periodic mixtures.** For primitive input period $d$, deterministic decoding has a recurrent cycle of at most $d$ boundary residues. If it contains both loads and pushes, its emitted length $n_P$ satisfies $0<n_P<t\le d$. An output equal to the periodic input would require $d\mid n_P$, a contradiction. Multiple allowed opcode identities preserve the argument. The degenerate all-push writer supplied an opcode-valued initial register is excluded. This proves an infinite periodic-stream obstruction for the stated single-register grammar, not absence of all finite 6502 writers. [results/literal_channel/CHECK.md; results/literal_channel/minimal_symbolic_search.csv; results/literal_channel/direct_search.csv]

The limits have constructive witnesses. At width three, `(abb)` repeated decodes as load `a[bba]` followed by two pushes and emits a matching shifted stream: the repeated-pair parity rule does not extend to mixtures. At width one, the archived finite-factor witness can match a finite rotated tape under prescribed periodic execution, yet fails zero-partner bootstrap when execution reaches unwritten memory. [results/literal_channel/verification.csv; results/literal_channel/finite_search.csv; results/literal_channel/finite_witness.json; results/literal_channel/6502_finite_harness.json] [fill: generated finite-witness/bootstrap summary table] Z80’s five nontrivial period-two writers likewise include only three literal channels; the remaining forms load from memory.
