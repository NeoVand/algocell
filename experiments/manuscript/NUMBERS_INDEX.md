### A. Numbers that do not match their source

1. **l. 25 (and letter, revision 3, item 5)**: "none of 512 mutant lineages still carries its mutation, with random
   partners or with cells of its own soup". 512 is the number of mutants of the 16-byte pusher (16 positions × 32 seeded
   values); each founds four branching populations, so there are 2,048 mutant populations. `results/branching/SUPP_TABLE.md`
   counts mutants with any population carrying the allele at g = 8: 0 of 512 in both arms. The soup arm draws partners
   from the step-5,000 and 10,000 snapshots of the 20 Stage G worlds at L = 16 (`BRANCHING.md`, arm K), not from the
   pusher's "own" soup. Consistent wording: "none of 512 single mutants (2,048 populations) still carries its mutation,
   with random partners or with cells of open-phase soups".
2. **l. 7 (summary)**: "Its lineages persist when they branch, but none keeps a mutation", in a sentence scoped to "tapes of
   16 to 64 bytes". True at L = 16 (unmutated populations alive at g = 8: 0.75 in both arms; 0 of 512 mutants carrying).
   At L = 64 (`SUPP_TABLE.md`): unmutated populations alive at g = 8 0.12 (uniform partners) and 0.44 (soup partners), and
   5 of 2,048 and 18 of 2,048 mutants still have a population carrying their allele at g = 8 (transmissible sites 0 in
   both arms by g = 4). Supported wording: "its lineages persist when they branch, but no site keeps a mutation".
3. **l. 13**: "recorded every encounter of 20 worlds to rebuild their genealogies". Forty worlds were recorded: seeds
   31001–31020 (`runs/lod_v6_modal/lod_v6/`) and 32001–32020 (N6, `runs/lod_n6_modal/lod_n6/`).
4. **l. 87 (Methods, Genealogies) and l. 240 (Supplementary Methods)**: "reproduced the recorded memories byte for byte in a
   validation world and all 20 registered worlds" / "the replay check held in all 20 registered worlds". It held in all
   40 recorded worlds: `V2_mismatches` = 0 in 20 of 20 `lod_v6` worlds (15,791,014 checked pairs) and 20 of 20 `lod_n6`
   worlds (15,883,358 pairs); `V3_max_changed_outside_pairs` ≤ 358 and ≤ 352 (computed from `runs/*/*/lod.json`).
5. **l. 37**: "usually as a rare product (in a median 0.2% and 0% of its encounters with soup partners)". The two medians
   are on different bases. First set: 0.0020 is the median of the 11 writers left after removing the producer 31005; over
   all 12 it is 0.0186 (`results/lod/FOUNDING_SUMMARY.md`). Second set: 0.0000 over all 11 and over the 9 left after the
   producers 32002 and 32015 (`results/lod_n6/FOUNDING_SUMMARY.md`). Consistent statement: "excluding three writers that
   make it with most partners (31005, 32002, 32015), a median 0.2% and 0%". In 12 of the 23 worlds (5 + 7) the writer makes
   the founder with none of 512 soup partners.
6. **l. 73 (Methods, Soup)**: "100,000 random and 9,000 targeted programs per size". `results/emulator/RING_DIFFTEST.md`
   reports two seeded runs, each of 100,000 random and 9,000 targeted programs at each of the six sizes (200,000 and 18,000
   per size in all). Say "in each of two seeded runs" or give the totals.
7. **l. 240 (Supplementary Methods, Genealogies)**: "Separating first founders from later conversions by existing closers
   identifies 15 later founders in 10 worlds, 11 of them with bytes from a confined copier." First set only
   (`results/lod/FOUNDERS2.md`); the N6 set has 14 later founders in 10 worlds, 11 of them with bytes from a confined copier
   (`results/lod_n6/FOUNDERS2.md`).
8. **l. 410 (Supplementary Information, Model 5)**: "in exploratory exact genealogies at L = 16 … on the lines of 17 closed
   worlds … in 12 worlds … in 5, and in 15 of 17 worlds its parts were one-byte variants of the pusher's own words", and

### B. Wording that overstates the source or names the wrong set, script or definition

11. **l. 25, l. 7 and l. 65**: "Against cells drawn from its own soup at step 5,000 the offspring still carry 3.8 bits …,
    although on the lattice a pusher's neighbour is its own kind in 57% of its encounters"; summary "makes copies that
    vary with it [its neighbour]"; Discussion "its children depend on the neighbour they were made against". X2's partners
    were drawn uniformly from the whole soup, where a median 0.15 of cells at L = 16 are ≥ 75% matches of the organism
    (`results/inflow_insoup/INFLOW_INSOUP.md`, kin share), whereas a lattice neighbour is kin (Hamming distance ≤ 2) in
    0.571 of encounters, and against kin the pusher copies in 0.979 and is damaged in 0.025 of encounters
    (`results/stageH/KIN_CENSUS.md`). Inflow among lattice neighbours was not measured (caveat recorded with the X2 outcome
    in `REVISION_PREREG.md`). The 57% is in Supplementary Methods (Controls, l. 262), not in the Methods the text cites.
12. **l. 25 and Supplementary Table 2 (and letter item 5)**: "48 of 64 unmutated lineages survive eight generations". The
    registered value (`BRANCHING.md`, X1-1). The independent audit recorded with the X1 outcome in `REVISION_PREREG.md`
    (hand-written; no generated table) put control survival at about 0.62 with 2,048 populations and said that arm K's
    survival is inflated by pool cells that already copy the parent (0.131 of pool cells at L = 16 and 0.239 at L = 64 are
    ≥ 75% copies of the wild type, `BRANCHING.md`) "so arm K's survival is not cited"; Supplementary Table 2 tabulates it
    (0.98 / 0.88 / 0.75 and 1.00 / 0.70 / 0.44) without that caveat.
13. **l. 3 (header) and l. 65 (Discussion)**: "replicated as registered on 20 more" / "in an analysis replicated as

    test 14 of the 37 writers copy the code they run (`FOUNDING_SUMMARY.md`, both sets); the same paragraph says "does not

16. **l. 87 and l. 240**: paragraph headings "Genealogies (exploratory)". Both paragraphs now cover the registered
    replication (N6) and the registered X3; only the definitions of the first set were settled after inspection.
