# Designer brief, round 6 (2026-10-12)

Three conceptual panels are reserved in `figures/out/v6/fig1` and `fig3`.
The data panels and their letters are fixed; the designer supplies these three
panels. Legends: `MAIN_v6.md`, Figure legends; intent: `JOINT_CHARTER.md` §7.

**House rules.** Compose at 180 mm double-column width; Helvetica-like sans,
6–7 pt preferred, never below 5 pt. Pale teal organism, grey partner, vermilion
cycle-closing instruction/arc, teal pointer, purple lethal tar. A loop is filled
vermilion; an open route is grey. Two saturated colours at most in a panel,
grey structure, uniform thin strokes and proper arrowheads. About twelve
elements per panel; abbreviated strips with an ellipsis; no byte listings,
sentences, pseudo-code, decorative processors or invented numerical claims.
Labels belong outside the drawing, with panel letters aligned across each row.

1. **Fig. 1a, the encounter and its contract.** One pale-teal focal strip and
   one grey partner strip meet on a shared ring. A neutral reset token and a
   bounded execution token enter from outside. Teal pointer traverses the ring;
   a writeback pair separates into focal and offspring, then an offspring
   sampling token exits. Distinguish supplied resources from inherited machinery
   with thin boundary brackets, not a software architecture chart. Approximate
   element budget: two input strips, ring, reset token, budget token, pointer,
   writeback arrow, two output strips, sampling token, two boundary brackets.
   Sources: encounter implementation and declared semantic contract; schematic,
   no empirical proportion or throughput implied.

2. **Fig. 1b, three reproductive mechanisms.** Three aligned abstract rows:
   open pusher, closed regenerator, closed transmitter. Each uses one focal
   strip, one partner strip and one route: grey pointer exits the first;
   vermilion filled return closes the latter two. Add one common role key with
   four small marks for instruction start, instruction-stream fetch, data read
   and write. A single introduced-variation token disappears in the regenerator
   row and survives in the transmitter row. About twelve primary elements;
   repeated marks are a vocabulary, not a byte-by-byte reconstruction. Do not
   imply that confinement proves zero inflow, or that an unmodified copy assay
   proves retention. Sources: historical/exploratory `results/concept/traces.json`
   and `results/crystal/roles_*.csv`, `provenance_*.csv`; exact records stay in
   the data archive. No byte strings in the drawing.

3. **Fig. 3a, overlap protects a prefix.** Two aligned copy geometries use the
   same compact vocabulary. Each has a short parental strip, a protected-core
   bracket, a read pointer, a displaced write pointer, and a descendant strip.
   Short displacement repeats the protected prefix; whole-parent displacement
   preserves independent payload positions. Two causal connectors complete
   roughly twelve elements. Repeated output cells must visibly share a source;
   independent source coordinates must remain distinct from repeated output
   positions. Keep both strips abstract with an ellipsis. Place `d`, `c` and
   `r = d − c` outside the drawing; no sequences, exhaustive ring, or numbered
   cells. The geometry is conditional on forward coverage, phase and protected
   core, as the legend states; it does not count working core alternatives.
